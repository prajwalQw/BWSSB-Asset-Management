Title: BWSSB turns to AI to cut power bills at pumping stations
Publication Date: January 15, 2026
Source: The New Indian Express, Bengaluru
URL: https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Jan/15/bwssb-turns-to-ai-to-cut-power-bills

BENGALURU: Faced with monthly electricity bills exceeding Rs 65-70 crore for lifting Cauvery water, the Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB) has deployed Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning diagnostics to optimize high-tension pumping station operations.

The pilot deployed at Cauvery Stage IV pumping stations (TK Halli, Harohalli, and Tataguni) evaluates pump operating efficiency, detects cavitation, and dynamically schedules pump dispatch to minimize peak-hour power tariff penalties.

Documented Telemetry & Diagnostic Parameters:
1. Pumping station power consumption (Active power kW, Apparent power kVA, Total energy kWh)
2. Pump motor vibration (triaxial velocity and acceleration for bearing wear detection)
3. Motor bearing temperature
4. Pump discharge pressure
5. Volumetric pump delivery rate / discharge flow
6. Dynamic pump efficiency calculation (wire-to-water efficiency index)
7. Peak-tariff load shift scheduling signals
