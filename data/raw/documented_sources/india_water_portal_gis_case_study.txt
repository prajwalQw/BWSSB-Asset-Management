Title: Using GIS in water management system: case study, BWSSB
Publisher: India Water Portal & Arghyam
URL: https://www.indiawaterportal.org/amp/story/environment/sustainability/using-gis-water-management-system-case-study-bangalore-water-supply-and-sewerage-board

The Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB) implemented the Bangalore Integrated System for Operation of Network (BISON) GIS platform to digitize its vast distribution and sanitary networks.

Key Documented Network Parameters & GIS Feature Classes:
1. Pipeline geometric alignment (centerline vector coordinates)
2. Pipeline material (Cast Iron CI, Ductile Iron DI, Mild Steel MS, Reinforced Cement Concrete RCC, HDPE)
3. Pipeline nominal diameter (mm)
4. Sluice valve, scour valve, and air release valve spatial locations and operational status
5. Underground drainage (UGD) manhole spatial locations, cover levels, and invert depths
6. District Metered Area (DMA) boundary polygons and supply sub-zone delineations
7. Service station jurisdictional boundaries
8. Consumer water connection consumer identification number (RR Number) parcel associations
