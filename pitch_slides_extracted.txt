=== SLIDE 13: 8. Revenue Model  ===
  - 8. Revenue Model
  - This section details the specifics of your income streams and how you will make money from this business opportunity.
  - Revenue Model: Pricing and Revenue Forecast across years and how it will grow.
  - Pricing Strategy: Explain your pricing logic (e.g., competitive, value-based) and state the price clearly (e.g., ₹499/month).
  - Revenue Streams: List all the ways you generate income. Be specific.
  - Examples: Subscription Fees, Direct Product Sales, Transaction Fees/Commission, Licensing, Freemium Model, Advertising.
  - Customer Lifetime Value (LTV): Briefly estimate how much revenue you expect from a single customer over their entire relationship with your business.
